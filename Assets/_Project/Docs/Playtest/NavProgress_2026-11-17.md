## Pallet jack: agreed model (Tad, 2026-11-17) and what is in code

Everything is the jack's NavMeshAgent. The driver deviates from it at two points only. First point, STAGING A PALLET:
1. Pick the lane, the cell and the slot (`TryResolveStagingTarget`), and CLAIM the lane (`InventoryService.TryClaimLane`) before driving.
2. NavMesh leg to the lane's pivot point: on the lane centre line, `StageAnchorOut` = 2.5 m past the open end (`LaneAnchorPoint`).
3. Turn on the spot to face away from the lane (checked: a jack not within 15 degrees of facing out is given up, never reversed in nose-first).
4. Reverse straight in until the tip pallet is on the target cell centre.
5. Lower the pallet, release it, raise the forks (`ForkLift`, 0.25 m), drive straight forward to the pivot point, release the claim.
6. Its own navigation takes over again (parks / next task). No extra scripted moves after the pivot.
While a driver holds a jack, `AiNavigation.DriverOwnsMovement` stops the jack's own patrol / stuck-rebake / walk-out logic.

Second point, PICKING CASES (Tad, 2026-11-17):
1. The jack navigates to the mouth of the first pick's aisle (in line with it), then up the aisle to the pick's anchor (`DriveBayLeg`).
2. At the anchor the jack is parallel to the aisle (`ComputeBayPark` heading = aisle travel). Inside an aisle the jack's yaw is held within
   `AisleHeadingMaxDeg` (25 degrees) of the aisle direction during every nav leg (`SteerJackYaw`); the agent no longer rotates it.
3. The selector steps off, walks to the face, picks, walks back, places the case; repeats for every case of the item.
4. Next stop: just ahead in the SAME aisle (up to `StraightHopBays` = 3 bays) = a straight transform move (`TryStraightHop`, guarded by
   `MachineClearance`). Anything else = navigation: out of the current aisle by its far end, to the next aisle's mouth, up to the anchor.
5. A jack found more than 0.5 m / 12 degrees from its stop between cases (hit by a reach truck etc.) drives back to the stop
   (`RecoverIfMovedFromPark`). `MheAvoidanceObstacle` is back on pallet jacks so other agents steer round a parked one.
6. Done picking: out of the aisle on navigation, to the lane's pivot point (the staging process above).
Unused now (kept, not deleted): the scripted aisle-turn code in `OrderSelectionTaskDriver.AisleTurns.cs` (`DriveAisleTurn`, turn markers).
Pick order: ascending slot name (`OrderPickPath.SequenceKey`: aisle, bay, level, position). Exception: `SpreadTripStarts` moves a trip's first stop past another jack working the same aisle.
Unverified in Unity: all of the above. The assembly Unity had compiled on 2026-11-17 00:11 predates it.
