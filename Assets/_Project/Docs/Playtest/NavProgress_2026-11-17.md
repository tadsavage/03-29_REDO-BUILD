# Nav progress notes, 2026-11-17

Direction (Tad): a stronger nav agent in place of point-to-point logic. Pallet jacks now sit on the NavMeshAgent with only their
task-specific motion scripted. Reach trucks and dock stockers still use the grid planner behind their switches (all OFF by default).

## Done

### Pallet jack strip-down (`OrderSelectionTaskDriver.PalletJack.cs`, `.AisleTurns.cs`)
- Removed the grid leg (`OrderSelectionTaskDriver.GridLeg.cs`) and the traffic / queue / pass / walk-out logic
  (`OrderSelectionTaskDriver.JackTraffic.cs`), with their .meta files.
- `DriveJackTo` is NavMesh-only (`JackNavLeg`). Only `JackFace` (turn in place) and `JackMoveStraight` (short slide) are scripted,
  and both step through `MachineClearance.StepBlocked`.
- Aisle turns go through `DriveJackTo` plus `JackFace`.
- Kept: pulling up to picks, picking cases, making the turns.

### Charging and parking (`MheParking.cs`, `EquipmentBattery.cs`)
- Jacks never take a station's left, right or front spot. Only the bay is used, so at most one jack is parked per station.
- Idle jacks on the floor keep 4.5 m (`JackStationClearance`) from any station centre.
- Charge trips accept a bay-centre tolerance of 0.3 m (`BayCentreTolerance`), with a two-try pre-leg to the bay.
- Other machines (reach trucks, dock stockers) keep their side and front spots.

### Rescue and logging (`OrderSelectionTaskDriver.PalletJack.cs`)
- `TryRescueStrandedJack` ignores Complete tasks.
- "rescue-noorder" and "rescue-notask" warnings are throttled to once per 60 s.

### Nav audit
- NavAudit re-run on the quicksave layout: 60/60 pick locations reachable by jack and reach truck on the NavMesh, 0 disagreements,
  goal shift 0.0 m (`Logs/NavAudit.txt`).

### Grid tooling
- `NavGridBuilder`, `NavGridDebug`, `NavGridMenu`: the jack switch is removed. The FlagSummary line reads `jacks=navmesh`.
- `EquipmentTrafficTests`: the `JackGoesFirst` antisymmetry test is removed (it referenced deleted code).
