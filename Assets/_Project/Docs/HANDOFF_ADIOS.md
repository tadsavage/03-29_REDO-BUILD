## Outbound clock + loader fixes (2026-11-03 night; compiled, deadline rule unit-checked, NOT yet seen in a real run)

It only ever moves LATER (the slot is still the customer's promise), so a driver held up at the gate/yard no longer eats the loading time. `DockAppointment.GatePassAbs` is saved (snapshot `gatePassAbs`; 0 in old saves = slot-only). Set by `TruckController.GuardClearedToEnter` -> `NoteGatePass`; `DockedAbs` by `OnDocked` -> `NoteDocked`. `GateToDoorMinutes` (72) now lives in DockScheduleService; the dispatcher uses it. ## FIXED, CRITICAL (2026-11-04, seen live by Tad): a jack drives itself to a charger with its selector mid-pick
What he saw: a pallet jack slid back out of the aisles to a charging bay without turning (Y rotation fixed), with Henry Robinson aboard, "Selecting orders", an order (OG2813) part picked (1/48 cases on the pallet). It was not low on battery.
Live read (editor, play): the jack was at 92% charge, `needsCharge=False`, yet `EquipmentBattery.OnChargeTrip` and `VacatingBay` were both true while the jack was claimed by Henry's driver (`IsJackClaimed`, `busy=True`). A minute later it read 100% and the trip flags were clear: it had charged, then released. His driver still held the order and the jack.
Likely cause (NOT yet reproduced or fixed): `EquipmentBattery.Update` sends a free employee to take an UNMANNED jack that is not on a charger to one (`StartHelperTask(true)` -> `VacateTrip`), and `FindVacateHelper` prefers an idle ORDER SELECTOR. That same selector can claim the same jack for a pick (`FindFreeJack`) before they board. `VacateTrip`'s wait loop only stops if the helper already has an assigned task; once `Slot.CurrentOperator == helper` it counts as boarded and starts `ChargeTrip` (hand-drive `MoveStraight`, no turning) regardless of the pick run. Two controllers, one jack.
Proposed fix (when Play is stopped): in `VacateTrip`, treat the boarding as the helper job's only if `!OrderSelectionTaskDriver.IsJackClaimed(Slot)`; abort the trip (clear `VacatingBay`, release the helper, `_wantPark = false`) when the jack is claimed by a pick run. Also make `FindFreeJack` skip a jack whose battery has `VacatingBay` set for someone else. Verify by reproducing with a selector idle next to an unmanned jack off its charger.

FIX (2026-11-04): `EquipmentBattery.VacateTrip` now refuses the errand when a selector's pick run holds the jack (`PickRunHasJack()`: `OrderSelectionTaskDriver.IsJackClaimed`), both while waiting for the helper to board and after boarding; the helper is released and `VacatingBay` clears. `OrderSelectionTaskDriver.FindFreeJack` skips a jack whose battery has `VacatingBay` set. Checked in the editor: with Henry's jack claimed by his pick run, asking its battery for a charge errand started no trip (before, it drove the jack away), and the jack stayed under the pick run. Not yet seen across a full block.
FIXED (2026-11-04), seen in the same test: two jacks ended parked at exactly the same spot, both reading `STA-B` (5.228, 1.114, 50.963). Cause: `PalletJackParkingSpot.IsFreeFor` only held a reservation for a jack on a charge trip or already near the bay, so a selector's jack still driving in from across the building looked "stale" and a second selector reserved the same bay. Now a reservation stays live while its jack is still claimed by a selector's run (`OrderSelectionTaskDriver.IsJackClaimed`). Checked in the editor by reserving a bay for a far-away jack: free for a second jack without a claim (the genuinely stale case), taken with a claim. Not yet seen across a full block with two selectors finishing together.
