## Addendum 2026-10-31 — morning commit (fd8fbdf58), dock-lane lock watchdog, Purchasing/Scheduler fixes (compiled; NOT play-verified)

- **Trailer attach is block-aware** (`DockScheduleService`): an arriving order attaches only to a trailer in its requested block (was day-only, so a 20:00 order could ride a 10:00 trailer). Reconciliation is block-aware too.
- **Recurring holds:** a recurring account keeps a hidden capacity hold (`DockAppointment.IsHold`) per delivery day instead of a visible empty trailer. The hold clears when its order attaches. Holds are not drawn on the Scheduler and are never dispatched (`OutboundTruckDispatchService` skips them). `OrderArrivalService.MaintainRecurringSchedule` clears old empty pre-bookings, then books holds.
- **No shared-lane last resort** (`StagingLaneAssignmentService`): no free lane means the order waits.
- **No SCRATCHES tasks for pre-arrival releases.** Waiting pallet picks stay Open until stock arrives (`OrderService`, `IsWaitingForStock`). RETURNS are unchanged.
- **Dock lane 1A deadlock fixed** (`ReachTruckOperator.CheckStuckTask`): the 120 s watchdog called `StopAllCoroutines()`, which never runs the routine's cleanup, so the lane-entry lock (`_heldLaneLock`) and the staging reservation stayed held. Every later delivery into that lane (dock stockers included) queued at the lane mouth forever. The watchdog now calls `ReleaseLaneLock()` and `ReleaseStagingReservation()` after stopping the coroutines. Rule: any code that stops a routine holding a lock or reservation must release it explicitly.
- **Purchasing (`PurchasingPanel`):** Potential Scratches nets basket quantities and on-order cases (`Economy().GetTotalOnOrder`), matching the Item Listing (`ItemAvailability.ShortCasesBySku`). After a PO dispatch the list re-sorts with the largest scratches first and the top filters still apply. The item checkbox filter keeps the vendor list where it is (`CaptureItemFilterScroll` / `RestoreItemFilterScroll`, one frame after the rebuild).
- **Scheduler (`SchedulerPanel` + `DockScheduleService.KeepPastDays = 1`):** yesterday stays on the Scheduler once as a "last look" with the red past-day fill across the lanes. It is purged at the next day rollover and cannot be paged back to.
- **Not verified in play:** every item above. Check the dock lane 1A delivery after a stuck reach truck, the Purchasing sort/filter after a dispatch, and the Scheduler's past-day red fill.

## Addendum 2026-10-31 (night) - Dig-out of buried trailers, machines never descend, ClickProbe (compiled; first real block = 27)

- **Dig-out of a buried trailer (Tad's rule: getting the trailer loaded outranks putaways).** `TrailerLoadController.Blockers.cs`: every 2 s `ScanForBlockedLanes` looks at each docked outbound trailer (`AwaitingLoad`) and, per lane where its orders' staged pallets stand, `PlanBlockers` lists the run of NON-own pallets (another order's staged pallet, or received stock) that stands in front of / on top of an own pallet, door end first, top first. If the loader cannot reach all its pallets from either end (`FindStagedPalletsInLane`) a `WorkTaskType.Dig` task (appended enum value, DockStockerOperator, priority 900, key `DIG:<lane>` in PalletId, transient: not saved) is filed and the Systems Log says so. `TryStartBlockerDig` runs at the TOP of `Update`, before any Load task, claims a free dock stocker and runs `BlockerDigRoutine`: pick the front blocker (`PickUpFromLaneEntry`), NavMesh round to an empty unowned lane's far end (`TryFindDepositSlot`), `SetDownFromFarEnd`, re-plan, repeat until the trailer's own pallet is first; then the ordinary Load task takes over. A foreign order's pallet moved this way gets the new lane added with `AddOverflowLane` so its own loader finds it. `_digLanes` keeps `LoadRoutine` out of a lane being dug. Failures put the task back with a 15 s backoff.
- **Reach trucks and blockers:** received stock that is in the way is published in `TrailerLoadController.BlockingStockPalletIds`; `ReachTruckOperator.TryClaimAndStart` takes those putaways before anything else (a blocker beats every priority). The dig plan stops at any pallet another vehicle is already assigned, so DS and reach truck never fight over one.
- **Machines NEVER descend.** Root cause of "fell below the floor" (MHEBoundary errors in blocks 19-26): the MHE NavMesh has a second, yard-level layer (y ~0.08) under and beside the building edge (measured live: 189 'inside' cells have one, incl. the cells of the reported falls). Any Warp / SamplePosition / rescue that lands on it dropped the machine 1 m. `AiNavigation.LateUpdate` now refuses to adopt a machine's `agent.nextPosition` when it is below `MachineMinFloorY` (0.9) and the machine was above it: it warps the agent back to a floor-level hit near the last floor position (`RejectYardLevelSnap`, throttled warning) or drops the leg. `MHEBoundary` records `_lastInside` only at y >= 0.9 and its recovery warp is floor-level-validated (it used to record 0.6-0.7 spots, i.e. already-sunk ones).
- **Click bug (avatar / equipment left-click):** could NOT be reproduced - with a real queued mouse click in Play the Employee Info card (with its live model) and the battery card both open, and picking/UI-swallow checks pass for every employee. New `UI_UX/ClickProbe.cs` writes ONE warning (5 s throttle) whenever a left click lands within 70 px of an employee or machine and is not over UI: what the click picker returned, the first 6 raw physics hits, the modal holder. Read `[ClickProbe]` in Logs/Editor.log after Tad hits it. A press must be preceded by a release when injecting test clicks (`wasPressedThisFrame`), else nothing registers.
- Tooling: Unity only recompiles while its window is focused; `AssetDatabase.ImportAsset(file, ForceUpdate)` + `CompilationPipeline.RequestScriptCompilation()` via `execute_code` forces it (a brand-new .cs file needs its .meta generated first or the first compile misses it). `execute_code` cannot define classes.
